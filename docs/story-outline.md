# Story outline: The Signal

**Status: approved 2026-11-16.** Missions are built from this; see the phase 6 document for progress.

## Premise

The Ashfall Marches are what is left after a war nobody now alive fought. The biggest thing it
left behind is the Fallen Carrier, a hull the size of a town that every faction has picked over
and written off as dead. Then the Listening Dish, silent for a lifetime, starts transmitting, and
something inside the Carrier answers. Whoever reaches it first gets whatever is still running in
there. All four factions heard it. So did you.

## Tone

Dry, working-pilot talk over the radio; a slow mystery under it. No chosen one: you are a
salvager who happened to be closest. The thing in the Carrier is neither a monster nor a prize,
and the ending is about who you hand it to.

## Characters

- **Magpie** (the player). An independent salvage pilot flying out of Drifters' Rest. Speaks in
  short comms lines: practical, a little wry, never a speech.
- **Tamsin Hale.** Runs the radio room at Drifters' Rest. Your dispatcher through the whole
  campaign, whichever side you take. Curious where you are cautious.
- **Harbor.** The custodial mind of the Fallen Carrier, and the source of the answer to the Dish.
  Polite, patient, decades out of date. Wants to finish the one job it was left: hand the ship
  over to a crew.
- **Commander Ilse Varga, "Kestrel One".** The Combine's ace, sent to secure the Carrier. Shadows
  you from the second mission and is the duel at the end. Professional, not cruel.
- **The patrons** (you pick one at the midpoint):
  - **Ojo Brandt**, quartermaster of the Drifter Compact. Wants the Carrier's power for the
    settlements: "Keep the lights on."
  - **Captain Brenna Sorrel** of the Greywall Wardens. Wants it sealed where nobody can use it:
    "Some doors stay shut."
  - **Lys**, a broker for the Emberline Syndicate. Wants to sell it, and will cut you in:
    "Everything has a buyer."

The Kestrel Combine makes no offer. They intend to take the Carrier, and are the opposition
whichever patron you choose.

## Missions

Six missions, one of each mission type, about two hours. The first three are the same for
everyone. The patron you choose after the third changes who briefs you, who fights beside you,
what you are paid in, and the ending. The places stay the same.

| # | Title | Type | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clear Signal | Assault | Kestrel Outpost, Ashfall Basin | The outpost is jamming Drifters' Rest. Clear it and come home. This is the slice mission, re-expressed. With the jammer down, Hale picks up a transmission that should not exist: the Listening Dish is sending. |
| 2 | Listening Post | Recon | The Listening Dish | Go and look without starting a fight. Tag three relay masts around the Dish while a Combine survey team works the site. Hale finds the Dish is not broadcasting, it is calling, and something at the Needle is relaying an answer. First sight of Varga, leaving. |
| 3 | The Needle | Defend | The Needle, Sunken Quarter | Hold the spire while Hale pulls the answer off its transmitter. Syndicate raiders, then the Combine, come in waves. The answer is a voice: Harbor, asking for a crew. It went out in the clear. Everyone heard it. |
| | *The offers* | | Drifters' Rest | Three messages are waiting at base: Brandt, Sorrel and Lys. Pick a patron. Only factions that are not hostile to you make an offer. |
| 4 | Dead Reckoning | Salvage run | Broken Span | Harbor will only open for its own command key, last logged aboard an escort that went down at the Span. Find the key among the wrecks and get out before the Combine's salvage teams finish closing the ring. |
| 5 | Long Walk | Escort | Broken Span to the Fallen Carrier | Your patron sends an engineer suit to carry the key and do the work. Walk it to the Carrier through Combine pickets. Your patron's pilots join for the last stretch. |
| 6 | Kestrel One | Duel | The Fallen Carrier | Varga is waiting at the hull, alone, with an offer to walk away. Then the duel. Afterwards Harbor opens, and you keep your word to your patron. |

## Endings

- **Drifters.** Harbor's reactor is tied into the Compact's grid. Harbor has a crew again, of a
  sort. Hale: "It keeps asking what watch it is."
- **Wardens.** Sorrel's engineers seal the hull. Harbor asks to be told when it is safe to wake,
  and Magpie says they will. Nobody believes it.
- **Syndicate.** Lys has a buyer before the dust settles. You are paid very well, and Hale does
  not speak to you on the walk home.

After the ending the world carries on: the war, contracts and free play continue.

## Rewards

Each mission pays scrap and standing with whoever you worked for. The patron choice gives a
large standing gain with that faction and a smaller loss with the other two who asked. The
last mission awards one named part that depends on the patron.

## What this needs from the game

- A friendly AI suit that follows a route (the engineer in mission 5).
- Objects to interact with (relay masts in mission 2, the key in mission 4).
- Waves spawned by a mission rather than the war (missions 3 and 4).
- A single named enemy with their own build (Varga).
- A choice screen at base for the offers.
